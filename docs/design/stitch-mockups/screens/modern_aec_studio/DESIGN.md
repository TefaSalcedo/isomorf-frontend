## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines technical structural engineering and seismic BIM modeling software through the lens of modern, approachable creative suites. Traditionally, CAD and finite-element modeling software has been utilitarian, overwhelming, and visually outdated. This design system bridges deep mathematical rigor and structural accuracy with the frictionless, delight-driven workflow of modern creative tools.

The brand persona is structured, mathematically confident, and refreshing. It addresses structural engineers, architects, and site inspectors who require high-precision tools without visual fatigue. The emotional response is one of clarity, mastery, and effortless flow: complex FEA calculations, load cases, and BIM spatial parameters feel tangible, tidy, and responsive.

The aesthetic merges **Modern Corporate SaaS** with **Soft Neo-Productivity**:
- Pristine white workspaces framed by clean, low-contrast neutral panels (`slate-50` to `slate-200`).
- High-saturation primary violet accents (`#7C3AED`, `#6D28D9`) that command primary user actions, step flows, and active structural selections.
- Soft lavender tints for selection highlights, secondary action containers, and floating canvas tools.
- Emerald green cues for verification states: zero-drift checks, successful cloud saves, code compliance, and structural safety confirmations.
- Ample negative space, generous rounded corners (`rounded-xl` and `rounded-2xl`), and subtle structural borders that maintain rigorous engineering clarity.

## Colors

The color palette centers on a high-energy structural violet complemented by soft structural lavenders, clean slate structural tiers, and an emerald verification green:

- **Primary Violet (`#7C3AED` / `#6D28D9`)**: Used for the primary call-to-action buttons (e.g., "Crear", "Analizar modelo"), active step indicators, selected structural elements in the 3D viewport, and active tab indicators.
- **Secondary Lavender (`#EDE9FE` / `#F5F3FF`)**: Soft tinted containers, secondary icon badges, active navigation menu backgrounds, and selected property tags.
- **Tertiary Emerald (`#059669` / `#10B981`)**: Signals validated structural safety, cloud synchronization ("Guardado"), compliant drift ratios, and completed calculation passes. Paired with soft tint `#ECFDF5`.
- **Neutral System (`slate-50` through `slate-900`)**:
  - `slate-50` (`#F8FAFC`): Global application background and panel backdrops.
  - `slate-100` (`#F1F5F9`): Inactive canvas pill controls, secondary button fills, and input backgrounds.
  - `slate-200` (`#E2E8F0`): Subtle panel separators, card borders, and tool grid boundaries.
  - `slate-500` / `slate-600` (`#64748B` / `#475569`): Unselected structural tool icons, breadcrumbs, secondary labels, and unit denominators.
  - `slate-900` (`#0F172A`): Primary headings, input text values, and active tool states.

## Typography

Typography balances clean geometry with hyper-legible engineering data readability.

- **Primary & Display Typeface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides welcoming, modern, human-centric geometry for top-level navigation, model titles, modal headings, and structural tool labels. Its open counters and geometric curves inject modern design tool aesthetics into structural software.
- **Precision & Data Typeface (`Inter`)**: Applied to all property values, engineering units (e.g., `kN/m`, `f'c = 28 MPa`, drift values `0.008 m`), form input controls, and breadcrumbs. Inter’s tall x-height and tabular spacing prevent visual jitter during real-time load adjustments.
- **Hierarchy & Case Usage**: Section headers in tool palettes (e.g., `DIBUJAR`, `ESTRUCTURA`, `CARGAS`, `ANÁLISIS`) use `label-sm` in all-caps with generous letter spacing (`0.04em`) in `slate-400` to anchor spatial organization without overpowering tool glyphs.

## Layout & Spacing

The workspace operates as a multi-panel productivity canvas optimized for dense desktop workflows with responsive collapsing capabilities.

### Layout Model
1. **Top Application Bar (`header-height: 4rem`)**: Spans 100% width. Houses document naming, cloud synchronization badges, global undo/redo, sharing, export actions, and user avatars.
2. **Global Activity Bar (`sidebar-nav-width: 12rem`)**: Leftmost vertical rail containing high-level workspace navigation (Inicio, Proyectos, Modelar, Analizar, Plantillas).
3. **Tool Shelf Panel (`tool-shelf-width: 13.5rem`)**: Secondary left rail housing categorized BIM structural primitives organized into clean vertical stacks with 3-column micro-grids for tools.
4. **Central 3D Viewport**: Fluid container stretching to claim all central space. Floating controls (2D/3D toggle, view orientation gizmos, zoom/pan dock) float over the canvas with `space-md` offsets from container boundaries.
5. **Right Inspector Panel (`inspector-panel-width: 20rem`)**: Dedicated property inspector for dimensional parameters, structural releases, and material properties.
6. **Bottom Workflow Stepper (`footer-bar-height: 4.25rem`)**: Floats or docks at the bottom of the central canvas, guiding the structural pipeline from drawing to analysis execution.

### Adaptation
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: All 4 panels active simultaneously with fixed widths and fluid 3D central viewport.
- **Tablet Landscape (1024px - 1279px)**: Tool shelf and right inspector convert into sliding overlay sheets triggered by floating action pills.
- **Small Screens (< 1024px)**: Viewport takes 100% width; side panels convert into bottom drawer modals.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered structural white planes, crisp 1px borders in `slate-200`, and soft, highly diffused ambient violet-tinted shadows rather than heavy dark drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Workspace Canvas)**: The 3D modeling viewport sits on a soft radial gradient from `#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Level 1 (Docked Surfaces & Panels)**: Activity bar, tool shelf, and inspector panels sit flush on `slate-50` or pure `#FFFFFF` separated strictly by `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Canvas Controls & Toolbars)**: Viewport controls (2D/3D switcher, zoom dock, bottom calculation wizard) float with `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.04)` and a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, and Tooltips)**: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.08)`.
- **Interactive State Elevation**: Hovered tool buttons and action tiles elevate with a subtle `-1px` transform and a gentle violet glow (`0 0 0 2px #EDE9FE`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces generous, modern curved corners that soften the technical complexity of civil and structural engineering:

- **Base Corner Radius (`rounded-md` / 8px)**: Internal input controls, dropdown select triggers, and property list tiles.
- **Component Corner Radius (`rounded-lg` / 12px - 14px)**: Floating viewport toolbars, tool selection tiles, standard buttons, and tab segment containers.
- **Container Corner Radius (`rounded-xl` / 16px - 20px)**: Dedicated workspace cards, property inspector wrapper, and floating bottom calculation bars.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full`)**: Status tags (e.g., "Guardado", "Seleccionado"), view mode switcher toggles (2D / 3D), and primary calculation execution pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action ("Crear", "Analizar modelo")**: Deep violet gradient fill (`#7C3AED` to `#6D28D9`), white bold text, `rounded-xl` or `rounded-full`, soft drop shadow `0 4px 14px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.35)`. Hover: `#5B21B6`.
- **Secondary Action ("Compartir", "Exportar")**: Crisp white background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, slate-700 text. Hover: `slate-50` background with `border-violet-300`.
- **Tool Palette Buttons**: 64x64px square tiles with `rounded-xl`, subtle slate-100 hover background, centered vector icon, and 11px label beneath. Active state features a soft violet background (`#EDE9FE`), a violet border (`#7C3AED`), and deep violet glyphs.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Verified / Saved Status**: Pill shape with `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` emerald text, and a leading checkmark icon.
- **Selection Badges ("Seleccionado")**: Pill shape with `#EDE9FE` background, `#6D28D9` bold text.
- **Segmented Canvas Switchers (2D / 3D)**: Encapsulated pill container in `#F1F5F9` with an active sliding pill in solid violet (`#7C3AED`) with white typography.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`.
- Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Focus state: `1px solid #7C3AED` accompanied by a 3px ring of `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)`.
- Label placed directly above in `slate-700` medium 12px typography; trailing engineering units (`m`, `kN`, `MPa`) formatted in `slate-400` tabular mono.

### Cards & Property Containers
- Card containers feature `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, and `rounded-2xl` corners.
- Contextual tip cards (e.g., "Sugerencias") utilize a very soft cyan/lavender tint (`#F0FDF4` or `#F5F3FF`), `#0284C7` or `#7C3AED` iconography, and light border accents to provide non-intrusive modeling guidance.

### Bottom Calculation Stepper
- A continuous horizontal floating pill bar containing sequential structural steps:
  1. *Dibujar* → 2. *Definir materiales* → 3. *Aplicar cargas* → 4. *Analizar* → 5. *Revisar* → 6. *Entregar*.
- Completed steps display emerald check badges; active steps display an encapsulated numbered purple disk with high-contrast white text.
